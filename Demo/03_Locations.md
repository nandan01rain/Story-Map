# LOCATIONS — THE SOUTHERN WING (DEMO)

---

## MADAMPALLI THARAVAD
**Type:** Primary setting — ancestral estate house, present day.
**Description:** Large multi-generational tharavad in rural Kerala. Central courtyard, verandahs, a household shrine, a portrait gallery of ancestors (one portrait conspicuously damaged/missing — plant, see Plants & Reveals), several wings added across generations by different branches of the family. Should read as a character in its own right — old enough that its layout encodes the family's history.
**Scenes set here:** Nearly all of Act One and Two; the ritual courtyard for Act Three is a purpose-dressed section of the main courtyard.
**Sub-locations:**
- **The main courtyard** — communal space, site of the Act Three ritual.
- **The portrait gallery** — corridor connecting the main house to the Thekkini's outer door; site of the Ch 4 "eyes that follow" incident.
- **The household shrine** — where the family's failed pujas take place (Ch 5).
- **Sridevi's quarters** — where she's confined in Ch 8.

## THE THEKKINI (Southern Wing)
**Type:** Forbidden interior location — locked wing of the mansion.
**Description:** Preserved almost exactly as it was 150 years ago. Dancer's anklets, an unlit oil lamp, a cracked mirror, personal effects belonging to Nagavalli. Has its own external door, separate from the main house, which is how Ganga and Alli can access it without crossing the household (Ch 3). Should feel arrested in time rather than overtly sinister — the horror is in the stillness, not decoration.
**Scenes set here:** Ch 3 (the unlocking), Ch 7 (the midnight confrontation), Ch 13 (briefly, before the action moves to the courtyard).

## THE COTTAGE (historical)
**Type:** Flashback-only location.
**Description:** A modest cottage adjoining the estate grounds, where Ramanathan lived to be near Nagavalli in secret. Site of his murder.
**Scenes set here:** Ch 0 only.

## EVOOR
**Type:** Secondary present-day location — a half-day's travel from the estate.
**Description:** A village home to descendants of the household's former retainer families, and to a temple holding a more accurate historical record than the family's own oral legend.
**Scenes set here:** Ch 9 (Sunny's investigative trip).

## THANJAVUR (historical)
**Type:** Flashback-only location.
**Description:** The royal court where Nagavalli and Ramanathan trained and performed before the Karanavar's visit. Seen only in Ch 0's opening movement.
**Scenes set here:** Ch 0 only.
